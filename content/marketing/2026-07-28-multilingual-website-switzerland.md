## YouTube Shorts script

One website can serve several Swiss markets.

But a multilingual website needs more than translated words.

First, choose languages based on real customer demand. Think about regions, enquiries and what your team can support well.

Second, give every language a clear home. Use predictable paths like slash de, slash fr, slash it and slash en.

Third, translate meaning, not just words. Adapt search wording, service terms and calls to action for each market.

Fourth, keep prices, forms, opening hours and legal information consistent across every language.

Fifth, use AI as an assistant. It can create drafts and compare versions. People should approve important content.

Finally, keep the system maintainable. Fewer complete language versions are better than many outdated ones.
