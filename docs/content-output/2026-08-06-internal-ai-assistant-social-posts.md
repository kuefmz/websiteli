## LinkedIn — English

An internal AI assistant can help a small business find approved procedures, summarise documents, prepare meeting briefs and draft internal responses.

But the useful question is not: “How much AI can we add?”

It is: “Which narrow, repeatable task should we improve first?”

A sensible first pilot usually has:

- one team
- one approved source collection
- one measurable task
- clear access rules
- source citations
- human review for uncertain cases

Many projects can use retrieval-augmented generation instead of training a custom model. The assistant searches approved company sources when a question is asked and uses the relevant information as context.

The difficult parts are often not the chatbot interface. They are document quality, permissions, ownership, testing and maintenance.
