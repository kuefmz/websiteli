# Websiteli 30-Day SEO Content Calendar

**Period:** 29 July 2026 to 27 August 2026  
**Primary market:** Switzerland  
**Primary languages:** English and German  
**Purpose:** Turn current Google Search Console visibility into qualified traffic and enquiries without flooding the site with low-value content.

## Strategy

The latest seven-day Search Console data shows early visibility around these topics:

- AI content pipeline
- Internal AI assistant
- Multilingual websites
- Online lead generation
- Website versus Facebook or social media
- Software development dashboards

The calendar therefore prioritizes:

1. Strengthening pages already receiving impressions.
2. Publishing commercially relevant Swiss content.
3. Building English and German topic clusters first.
4. Creating internal links to services, pricing, portfolio, demos, and contact.
5. Reusing every major article across LinkedIn and Instagram.

## Publishing Rules

- Publish no more than two substantial new articles per week.
- English is the source language. German should be manually reviewed before publication.
- Do not create all supported locale translations immediately unless the workflow requires them technically.
- Every article must include a clear commercial next step.
- Do not invent testimonials, rankings, client results, revenue impact, or case studies.
- Link each new article to two to five relevant internal pages.
- Add links from at least two older pages back to each important new article.
- Use real screenshots, diagrams, tables, or examples where they improve understanding.

## Measurement

Track these weekly for each priority page:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Average position
- Number of ranking queries
- Internal links added
- Organic visits in GA4 when available
- Contact or pricing-page visits originating from the article

Do not judge an article after only a few days. Use the first month to detect whether Google is increasing impressions and testing the page for relevant queries.

## Success Criteria After 30 Days

The plan is successful when:

- Three to four commercially useful articles are published or substantially improved.
- English and German priority content is available and manually reviewed.
- Every priority article has clear internal links and a CTA.
- Existing pages around positions 20–60 show stronger relevance signals or more impressions.
- The next calendar is based on Search Console evidence rather than generic topic generation.
