# Tailored proposal workflow

Public pages invite a consultation and do not advertise service prices. Retain the existing rates in `src/config/pricing.json` and `src/config/maintenancePricing.json` as the existing estimation reference; they must not be imported by browser runtimes or serialized into public pages. The historical configuration remains in source control. This repository is public, so it is not a confidential pricing store: confidential future rates belong in access-controlled business records.

1. Record the client's objectives, current systems, required outcomes, constraints, stakeholders and preferred timeline. Do not require a budget to start a conversation.
2. Separate discovery, design, engineering, integrations, content, migration, testing, launch and handover. Estimate each workstream consistently using the current approved internal rates and documented assumptions.
3. Record page/content volume, language responsibilities, data quality, systems access, third-party services, approval rounds and dependencies. Define ongoing support separately.
4. Review estimates against previous comparable work and delivery capacity. Record estimation assumptions and uncertainty; clarify unknowns before committing to them.
5. Provide a written proposal naming deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, milestones, acceptance criteria, fees, payment schedule and any third-party or recurring charges. Explain the next step and change-control process clearly.
6. Obtain scope and terms approval before implementation. Record changes and obtain agreement before additional work.
7. At handover, review delivery against the estimate, document the systems and ownership, and agree support responsibilities. Use the comparison to improve future estimates.

No minimum budgets, eligibility restrictions, guaranteed results or artificial scarcity are part of this process.
