# Blog Image Audit - September 2026

Audit date: 2026-09-07

Follow-up update: 2026-09-08

Scope: every published blog URL rendered by the current Astro blog source of truth across all languages.

## Summary

- Audited 280 localized blog article URLs: en 28, de 21, hu 21, pl 21, es 21, fr 21, it 21, cz 21, sk 21, pt 21, da 21, nl 21, ja 21.
- Audited 28 unique rendered blog images and checked the actual image assigned to each article.
- Replaced 8 article image mappings that were incorrect, generic for the topic, or duplicated across unrelated posts.
- Optimized 16 large PNG-based article images to 1536x1024 WebP companions while retaining the original files.
- Reduced unique rendered blog-image asset weight from about 28.64 MB to 2.57 MB, a roughly 91% reduction.
- Reworked the 6 posts published after 2026-08-05 into clearer conversion-oriented title-card images, while leaving the older post images unchanged.
- Added dedicated 1200x627 JPEG social cards for those 6 newer posts so OpenGraph, Twitter and Pinterest share media can use a LinkedIn-friendly preview while article/card images stay as optimized WebP.
- Confirmed all article hero images have non-empty alt text. Several shared cross-locale assets still contain concise English visual text, but the images are topic-relevant and no new creative assets are required for launch.

## Method

- Inspected the blog image source of truth in `src/content/blog/index.ts`, `src/content/blog/index-base.ts`, the individual post files, the blog listing component and the article template.
- Created local contact sheets from the real assets in `public/assets/blog` and visually inspected the article/image relationship, polish, crop safety and click appeal.
- Built the site with `npm run build`.
- Programmatically validated 280 built article pages for hero image source, alt text, dimensions, eager loading, fetch priority, OpenGraph, Twitter and schema image consistency.
- Programmatically validated all locale blog indexes for expected card links and card image references.
- Browser smoke-checked the blog listing and priority article pages at desktop and narrow mobile widths to confirm card thumbnails and article heroes render without obvious crop or overlap issues.
- For the 2026-09-08 follow-up, visually checked the new WebP and LinkedIn JPEG cards, verified the generated article HTML points OpenGraph/Twitter image tags at the `-linkedin.jpg` variants, and reran `npm run build`.
